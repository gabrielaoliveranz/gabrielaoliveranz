Hi, I'm Gabriela Olivera, an independent data analyst based in New Zealand, helping small businesses ask their numbers a question — and actually get a clear answer.

### About

I run a small data consultancy in Mount Maunganui working with New Zealand SMEs and primary-sector businesses. Most of what I build sits in the practical layer between scattered spreadsheets and a clear decision: cleaning messy exports, reconciling Xero reports with operational logs, and building repeatable models that don't break the next time someone adds a column.

I work across Python, SQL, Power BI, and Excel (Power Query / DAX), and have a strong bias toward tools a business owner can actually open, understand, and maintain without needing a full-time data team. If a dashboard needs a 20-page manual to explain itself, I simplify it until it doesn't.

Working hours are NZST/NZDT (UTC+12/+13).

### Currently working on

- **Small business analytics.** Fixed-price Excel and Power BI reporting systems that replace manual copy-pasting with automated Power Query pipelines and clean data models.

### How I work

- **Clarity over complexity.** A well-structured Excel or Power BI model that a team uses every Monday beats an over-engineered pipeline nobody touches.
- **Document the logic while it's fresh.** Every transformation, assumption, and scoring rule is written down so the numbers remain auditable six months later.
- **Small, verifiable steps.** Reconcile the totals at every stage of the pipeline before designing a single chart.

---

### Data privacy & handling

- **Zero client data in public repos.** Everything on this profile uses public New Zealand datasets or synthetic records. Client spreadsheets and Xero exports stay strictly within the client's own environment.
- **Local-first & minimal exposure.** Models are built to run inside the tools the business already owns (Excel, Power BI) without routing sensitive financial or operational records through unnecessary external servers.
